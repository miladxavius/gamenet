How to play

  • Drag gems onto the board. Fill a whole row or column to clear it.
  • You start with one kind of gem. Clear the whole board to unlock a new kind, up to seven.
  • Rocks grow on the board by themselves. Clear a line through a rock to break it.
  • Clear a line of gems that are all the same kind to get a TNT. Drop it anywhere to blast a 3×3 area.
  • Rocks show up after your first board clear, and the game slowly gets harder. It ends when nothing fits.

Big bonuses for breaking every rock on the board, and for clearing the whole board.
